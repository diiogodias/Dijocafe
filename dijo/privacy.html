Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy explains how DIJO Café & Delicatessen ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses information when you visit dijocafe.co.uk.

Who we are

What data we collect

This website does not ask you to enter any personal information. If you accept cookies, we collect anonymous information about how the site is used, such as which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are located, and the type of device and browser used.

If you contact us directly by phone or email, we will use your details only to respond to your enquiry.

Sharing your data

Your data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Cookies and Google Analytics

We use a cookie called dijo_cookie_consent to remember whether you have accepted or declined cookies, so we don't ask you again on every visit.

If you click "Accept", we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. Google Analytics sets cookies (named _ga and _ga_*) and processes this usage data on our behalf. If you click "Decline", Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. You can read how Google handles this data in the Google privacy policy.

To change your choice, clear the cookies for this site in your browser and the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request that it is deleted at any time by emailing dijocafedeli@hotmail.com. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Governing law

This policy is governed by the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.
